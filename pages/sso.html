Summary

What we're testing and for whom.

We're testing whether DaSSO, a self-hosted Keycloak server, can give the DAS team single sign-on across four internal apps: CaleChip, Ricotdin, SeatMGT and CertTracker. People sign in once with their email, company account, Google or GitHub, confirm with multi-factor authentication (MFA), and every connected app trusts that sign-in.

Problem

Today, every app keeps its own accounts.

Too many passwords

Each app needs its own account. Passwords get reused or forgotten, and reset requests end up with IT.

What it does

What changes for the team.

  • Sign in once

    After your first sign-in of the day, every connected app opens without asking again. Apps never see your password.

  • Sign in your way

    Use your email or company account, or link your Google or GitHub account through DaSSO.

  • MFA everywhere

    Set up multi-factor authentication once in Keycloak, and it protects every app behind it.

  • Remove access in one place

    Disable an account in Keycloak and it stops working across all four apps.

  • Permissions from groups

    Put someone in the admin or viewer group, and apps give them the matching role automatically.

  • One record of sign-ins

    Every sign-in, failure and admin change is logged in one place, which helps when something goes wrong.

How it works

What happens when you sign in. Try it in the demo below.

  1. You open an app

    Say you open CaleChip. It sees you aren't signed in and sends your browser to DaSSO.

  2. You sign in at DaSSO

    Use your email or company account, or pick Google or GitHub, then confirm with MFA. DaSSO starts a session for you.

  3. DaSSO vouches for you

    It sends the app a short-lived code. The app trades that code for a signed token directly with DaSSO, using OpenID Connect with PKCE.

  4. The app checks the token and lets you in

    It verifies DaSSO's signature, confirms the token was issued for it, reads your groups, and starts its own session.

  5. The next app opens straight away

    Open Ricotdin, SeatMGT or CertTracker next. DaSSO recognises your session, so each app gets its own token without asking again.

Conclusion

What we expect, and what it asks of us.

DaSSO gives the DAS team one secure sign-in for all four apps. It runs on Keycloak, so there is no license cost.

For the team
One account and one two-step setup instead of four separate logins.
For security
Two-step verification everywhere, and access removed in one place when someone leaves.
For us to own
Keeping Keycloak updated, backed up and monitored, and setting up each app carefully.
