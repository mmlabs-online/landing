00:48What it does
Meet Ricotdin

One browser tab turns talk into notes you can search and act on.

Open Ricotdin when your meeting starts. It captures the call's audio along with your microphone — audio only, no video — and once you stop, it does the rest on its own.

You get a speaker-labeled transcript with clickable timestamps, a summary and structured notes, the to-dos and dates people mentioned, and a chat that only answers from what was actually said. It works with Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom, because it never has to join the meeting — it just listens to your tab.

Features

Browser recording

Mixes the call and your mic into one audio-only track. Video is dropped before anything is saved.

Speaker-labeled transcript

Summary & notes

A short summary plus structured notes: topics, decisions and next steps.

To-dos & calendar

Action items with owners and dates, plus .ics export. Ricotdin won't make up a time nobody mentioned.

Chat with your meetings

Meeting library
