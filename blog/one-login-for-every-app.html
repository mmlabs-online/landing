Think about how many times you typed a password this week. One for email, one for the calendar, one for the tool your team switched to last month, and one more for the tool you forgot you had an account with. Each sign-in only takes a few seconds, but together they add up to a small, constant tax on your attention.

At mmlabs we make small apps, each built for one part of the workday. That approach has a catch: the more focused the apps are, the more of them you use. If every one of them asks you to sign in separately, the thing that makes them pleasant to use quickly turns into the thing that makes them annoying.

Small apps only feel small if switching between them costs nothing.

One account, every app

So we are building Authen, a single mmlabs account that works across all of our apps. The idea is simple: you sign in once, and every mmlabs app already knows who you are.

  • Open CaleChip in the morning to look at your calendar.
  • Jump into Ricotdin before a meeting without being asked to log in again.
  • Check SeatMGT or CertTracker later in the day, same account, same session.

One account also means one place to change your password and one place to sign out of everything at once.

What stays separate

A shared account is about who you are, not about everything you do. Each app still keeps its own information in its own place. Your calendar stays in CaleChip, your recordings stay in Ricotdin. Signing in once does not mean every app can see everything.

🔑

In short: one account to get in, separate apps for separate jobs. You get the convenience of a single sign-in without putting all of your work in one basket.

Where we are today

We want to be straight about this: one login across all apps is something we are building now, not something that is finished. Authen exists, and we are connecting the apps to it one at a time. Until an app is connected, it keeps its own sign-in.

We will post an update here each time an app joins the shared account, so you always know which ones are ready.

Why it matters for what comes next

The mmlabs app family will keep growing. Building the shared account now means every new app can plug into the same sign-in from its first day. You will never need a new account just to try the next thing we make.

If there is something about signing in that bothers you today, tell us through the contact form on our home page. It will shape how we build this.
